# Bodega pay later — handoff for the next builder (written 21 September 2026)

Peter's goal, in his words: «i just wanna sign bodegas up when i wake up» and «the key thing is
getting companies on waitlist and seeing if they refer other bodegas and suppliers». Everything
below is ordered by what serves that.

## What is live

- The site, soyrocoto.com (repository Rocoto-Labs/site, branch main). Spanish at `/` (Buying) and
  `/selling`; English under `/en`; Spain under `/spain/`; a language control beside the act.
  English is the source: `tools/peru_i18n.py extract | build | check` generates `peru/es/*.html`
  from `peru/i18n/es/*.json` and fails closed. `vercel.json` is generated by
  `tools/switch/make_vercel_json.py`; `tools/switch/verify.py` emulates Vercel's routing;
  `tools/route_check.py` is the live route gate (it fingerprints the first 4 KB of each page, so
  it proves routes and not copy).
- The sign-up, demo.soyrocoto.com/bodega-pay-later/ (this prototype, published from main by CI).
  Spanish by default, English with `?lang=en` (the English site links that way). The walk:
  arrival → connect WhatsApp → «¡Ya estás en la lista de espera!» (the pass lands, confetti) →
  what the business is → its name → its address (autocomplete, full-screen map) → where orders
  appear → businesses that have owed you (sellers only) → the referral door (every 3 businesses
  that join cover one more purchase) → done, «Salúdale a Rocoto en WhatsApp». Words live in
  `start/words.ts`. Yape left the walk on Peter's word; `#/start/yape` still opens and the
  status page links to it.
- The status page, `?lista=<CODE>` → `#/lista/<CODE>`: public, no cookie; the pass, the place, who
  joined through the link, the three seats, share, say hi, connect Yape.
- The buddy, in `mock-api/app/bodega.py`: on a shop's first WhatsApp message it sends the status
  link, then «Aquí abajo te mando el mensaje…», then the forwardable message on its own; later
  messages get the status link at most every ten minutes; a shop that stays silent five minutes
  after `POST /api/bodega/onboarded` gets the `waitlist_hello` template once. Every send goes to
  the business's own number, spends the daily ceiling and honours `ROCOTO_EGRESS_SANDBOX`.

## Before the buddy answers real people (in this order)

1. The demo host's env (`/srv/rocoto/demo.env` on rocoto-staging) already carries
   `BODEGA_WA_VERIFY_TOKEN` (vault item «Bodega WhatsApp webhook») and `BODEGA_WA_APP_SECRET`
   (copied on the host from `/srv/rocoto/.env`), beside `WA_CLOUD_PHONE_ID` and `WA_CLOUD_TOKEN`.
   Restart `rocoto-kit-runtime.service` after a publish if the endpoint answers 503.
2. ⚠ DO NOT change the Meta APP's callback URL. `/srv/rocoto/.env` holds the same app's
   `WA_CLOUD_VERIFY_TOKEN`, so the maintained backend already receives that app's webhooks, and
   an app has one callback. Route ONLY Rocoto's Peru WhatsApp account to the buddy with the
   account-level override: `POST /{WABA_ID}/subscribed_apps` with `override_callback_uri=
   https://demo.soyrocoto.com/api/bodega/wa/webhook` and `verify_token=<the vault value>`, using
   the system-user token in the vault item «Meta WhatsApp Rocoto USA number» (WABA
   2033396113925190). Check `GET /{WABA_ID}/subscribed_apps` before and after.
3. Submit the template: `messages/submit_templates.py --only waitlist_hello` with `META_WABA_ID`
   and `META_SYSTEM_USER_TOKEN`; record Meta's answer in MESSAGES.md's receipt. Its URL button may
   be recategorised as MARKETING; it is submitted as UTILITY with `allow_category_change`.
4. Prove it with a real phone: sign up, tap «Salúdale a Rocoto», expect the three messages in
   order; sign up again and stay silent, expect the template after five minutes.

## Laws that bind every word

Never a negative; a heading names the thing; Peter's own lines ship verbatim («Paga con crédito,
desde WhatsApp», «Cobranza directa por Yape», «Únete a la lista de espera»). Credit advertising in
Peru: no rate, fee figure, amount, term, date or promise of approval; planned things read as
planned. Spanish is Peruvian, «tú», warm and blunt — Monzo's voice as a Lima shopkeeper would say
it. The app never sends anything to a third party by itself. Never commit in this repository while
another worker edits the worktree (the pre-commit hook stashes). Production is reached only through
the tagged release chain.
