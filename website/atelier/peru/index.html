Questions

About connecting WhatsApp, supplier payments and credit.

What happens when I connect my WhatsApp?

WhatsApp integration connects business conversations to orders when configured by the shop.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Rocoto connects to WhatsApp Business, the free app for businesses. You can transfer your existing number and chats to it.

How does Rocoto connect to my WhatsApp?

Rocoto connects through the official WhatsApp Business integration. Your app keeps working on your phone.

When will credit be available?

Credit is planned and subject to lender assessment and approval.

How do I repay my credit?

The planned flow presents the repayment amount, date and lender terms before confirmation. The lender assesses and approves each application.

Does my supplier have to use Rocoto?

Your supplier registers the account where they want to receive payment and accepts the terms once. Payment confirmations then arrive in your chat with them.

What does Rocoto read from my WhatsApp?

Your individual conversations from the last 6 months. Group chats keep working as usual, and broadcast lists become read-only.

Is Rocoto a financial institution?

Credit is planned and subject to lender assessment and approval.
