Privacy

Rocoto reads your business's WhatsApp to write down what you buy and what you sell. This page says what it reads, what it keeps, who else sees it, and how you take it back.

Peru · Ley N° 29733 · Last updated 20 September 2026

Who Rocoto is

Rocoto is run by Leo Casa Limited, an English company registered under number 16259714, with its office in London, United Kingdom. Leo Casa Limited decides what happens to the data on this page.

Write to us at hello@soyrocoto.com.

What connecting your WhatsApp lets Rocoto read

You connect the WhatsApp of the business you run. From then on Rocoto reads the chats where you buy and where you sell: the orders, the totals your supplier sends you, the confirmations, and the photographs of the invoices you take inside the app.

It reads them for one job: to write down each purchase and each sale, so your whole history sits in one place and leaves as a PDF whenever you ask for it.

What Rocoto keeps

  • Your business — its name, its WhatsApp number, and the person who connected it.
  • Your book — every purchase and every sale Rocoto wrote down: who, what, how much and when.
  • Your pictures — the invoices and receipts you photograph inside the app.
  • Your place on the waitlist, and the businesses you brought with you.

Who else sees it

These are the companies Rocoto runs on. Each one works under contract and on our instructions.

Your book is used to run Rocoto for you. It stays with us and with the companies on this list.

Where it lives

Your book rests in the European Union, in Hetzner's data centre in Nuremberg, Germany. Some of the companies above are in the United States, and those transfers travel under the contracts Peru's rules ask for.

How long Rocoto keeps it

Your book stays while you use Rocoto. When you leave, we export it for you and delete it, and what Peru's tax and accounting rules ask us to hold stays for the time they ask.

Your rights under Ley N° 29733

Peru's Ley N° 29733 and its reglamento give you four rights over your own data: to see it, to correct it, to have it deleted, and to object to what we do with it.

Write to hello@soyrocoto.com from the number or the email you use with Rocoto, and we answer inside the time the law gives us.

If our answer leaves the matter open, you can take it to the Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales, at Peru's Ministerio de Justicia y Derechos Humanos.
