Terms

What Rocoto does for your business, what you agree to, what it costs, and how you leave.

Peru · Last updated 22 September 2026

This text is a draft, under review by our lawyer in Peru.

What Rocoto does today

You can join Rocoto's waitlist by connecting your business's WhatsApp. We will let you know when the services below are ready to try.

What is coming

Rocoto is preparing a simple register for counter sales and WhatsApp orders, with a record of each sale, payment and outstanding balance.

Auto-collect with Yape arrives soon, for a business that gives credit to other businesses. It is free.

The digital receipt — the boleta digital — arrives soon.

Rocoto is preparing working-capital credit for businesses like yours, with a financial institution supervised by the SBS. Businesses that already use Rocoto go first, and each business you bring moves you up the list.

What you agree to

  • You connect the WhatsApp of a business you run.
  • You keep your access to yourself, the way you keep the keys to your shop.
  • What you put into Rocoto is yours, and you let Rocoto read it to do the work above.
  • The chats Rocoto reads carry other people's names and numbers, and you stay responsible for them the way you are today.

What it costs

Joining the waitlist is free, and auto-collect with Yape is free when it arrives.

What anything else costs is agreed with you before it starts.

Early signup offer

Businesses that join the waitlist before credit launches can use the 0% interest offer on their first three loans when the service opens. Each loan is for up to S/500, with 7 days to repay. Rocoto covers the interest and fees for those seven days, so you repay the amount borrowed.

For example, borrow S/500 and repay S/500 after seven days: S/0 in interest and S/0 in fees for you. Each loan is subject to the financial institution's credit assessment and approval.

The 0% offer describes the cost to you after Rocoto covers the interest and fees. The financial institution, its contractual rates and the full credit terms are shown before you accept each loan. Credit is in preparation. We'll let you know when it is available.

How you leave

Write to hello@soyrocoto.com. We export your book for you and delete it, and what Peru's tax and accounting rules ask us to hold stays for the time they ask.
